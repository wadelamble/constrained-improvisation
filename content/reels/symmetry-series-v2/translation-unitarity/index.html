About the comparison

Each pair consists of two multiples of the same exponential mode. Their coefficients c₁ and c₂ are their values at x = 0. The displayed inner product is c1* c2 in this coefficient space, where the star means complex conjugation. It measures their magnitudes and relative phase.

This tests the multiplying factor by which an exponential mode responds to translation. The complex pair uses k = 2π/3, so a leftward shift of three units makes one complete turn. The radii and relative angle stay fixed throughout, not just at the end.

The real exponentials are a formal comparison, not normalizable wave packets on the whole line. Ordinary translations of real wave packets also preserve their inner product. The quantity displayed here is not an integral over the cropped plotting window.
