# Instagram hashtag discovery audit

Research date: 2026-09-17. Account: @symmetryphysics. This is a recommendation record; it does not modify published captions.

## Selected set

The user selected the same five tags for all 13 reels: **#Physics #ScienceExplained #FourierTransform #NaturalPhilosophy #QuantumMechanics**. Remove the project-name tag and #Mathematics. #NaturalPhilosophy replaces #WavePhysics to express the project's broader inquiry; its discovery performance remains unverified.

Publishing progress is tracked separately in `.tools/buffer-staging/text-updates/all-reels-tags/all-reels-update.json` and `native-verification.json`. This document records the choice, not completion of the live edits.

## Initial research recommendation

Keep #Physics, #Mathematics, and the existing series tag #ConstrainedImprovisation. Add #ScienceExplained and a subject tag that changes with the episode: #FourierTransform, #WavePhysics, or #QuantumMechanics. #QuantumPhysics is an alternative broad quantum tag worth testing, but the sampled content is more mixed.

This ranks candidates for a discovery test using observed audience/content fit. It is not a ranking of measured incremental reach or search volume.

These public pages are curated topical selections, not exhaustive or chronological hashtag feeds. Some entries do not contain the page's corresponding hashtag. The positive examples below explicitly contain the named tag. I did not treat the page's displayed total or an individual reel's views as hashtag search volume or evidence of causal reach.

#LearnPhysics, #PhysicsExplained, #NaturalPhilosophy, #MathematicalPhysics, and #WaveMechanics did not yield enough accessible primary evidence to recommend over the candidates above. Login redirects and weak indexing do not establish lack of activity. #TheoreticalPhysics returned a topical page of Planck stories, without corresponding literal hashtag use in the inspected entries; that page was not used to infer the hashtag's audience. #ScienceEducation similarly returned broad school-science topics without the literal tag in the inspected sample.

#ConstrainedImprovisation and #Mathematics were in the original set. The user subsequently chose to remove both, as recorded above. No claim of existing discovery demand for the project-name tag was made; this audit did not establish #Mathematics as a superior reach choice.

## What remains unmeasured

- Actual Instagram searches for each tag.
- Incremental reach caused by a tag, rather than by the reel, account, posting time, or recommendation system.
- Whether these audiences become readers of the manuscript.
- Performance of genuinely comparable small/new accounts: the accessible examples did not establish a reliable cohort.

For future posts, record caption/tag set, topic, publication time, duration, and comparable-age results (for example, 48 hours and seven days). Use non-follower reach where Instagram exposes it, and follows, saves, and shares alongside views. Compare subject tags within the same topic family; do not compare Fourier versus quantum posts as though tags were the only difference. With this small series, results will be suggestive rather than a controlled causal test. No ongoing monitoring or posting experiment has been scheduled.
